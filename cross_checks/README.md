# External cross-checks

Comparisons of this analysis against published work that measured the same
quantities by different routes. Kept separate from the work-package reports
because they serve a different purpose: **the WP reports say what we did, these
say whether anyone else agrees.**

Every check here is against a number we did **not** tune to and, in most cases,
did not look at until after the quantity was measured. Where that is not true it
is stated in the document.

## Why these three

They probe three genuinely independent layers of the chain:

- **Härer** tests the *output* — the supernova rate and the stellar mass that
  produces it. This is the closest thing the project has to an end-to-end check.
- **Orellana** tests the *astrometric foundation* — the systemic proper motion
  used in WP6's runaway traceback, and the distance that sets every absolute
  magnitude in WP3/WP4.
- **Berlanas** tests a *structural assumption* — that Cyg OB2 is one population
  along the line of sight, which WP2 relies on.

A check that agrees is worth little if it could not have disagreed. Each
document therefore states what a *failure* would have looked like.

## Standing rules for anything added here

1. **Quote the source verbatim** where the comparison turns on a number or a
   unit. Two of the three checks below hinge on exactly that.
2. **State what the check cannot establish.** The Berlanas comparison is the
   important case: our test is partly circular and the document says so.
3. **Never tune to a cross-check.** These are validations, not calibrations. If
   one disagrees, it becomes an issue in
   [PROJECT_TRACE.md](../PROJECT_TRACE.md) §9, not a reason to move a number.
4. **Record the direction and size** of any offset as a systematic, even when
   the check passes.
