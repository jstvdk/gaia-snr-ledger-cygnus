# WP4 — Subgroup ages and per-star masses

**Status:** WP4_COMPLETE — gate passed (see §7).
**Date:** 2026-07-23 · DR3 · distance fixed at **1.6245 ± 0.045 kpc** (μ = 11.054, single population).
**Isochrones:** the frozen WP3 grids (`wp3_isochrones_parsec.parquet`, `wp3_isochrones_mist.parquet`), solar Z, 1–10 Myr, Gaia G/BP/RP + 2MASS JHK — identical files used by WP3 (plan consistency requirement; not refetched).

> **Headline.** The three kinematic subgroups (A/B/C from WP2) are **consistent with being coeval at ≈ 3.5–4.5 Myr.** The CMD does **not** robustly resolve an age *difference* between them: subgroup A comes out marginally oldest (upper-MS ≈ 4.5 Myr in PARSEC) and B/C slightly younger (≈ 3.6–4.0 Myr), but the A–vs–BC gap (~0.5–0.9 Myr) is no larger than the PARSEC↔MIST model systematic (~0.9 Myr) and the ±0.045 kpc distance systematic (0.4–0.9 Myr). Per the WP4 gate we therefore report this as the **coeval result**, not a forced age spread. A genuine *within-association* age-spread signal does appear — the pre-main-sequence turn-on runs ~1.5 Myr younger than the upper main sequence in subgroup A — which is why the **star-formation-duration branch (0/1/2 Myr) is carried into WP7 regardless** (see §6).

## 1. Method (forward-model CMD likelihood)

Age is read from the de-reddened CMD by a **forward-model likelihood**, not by eye and not by nearest-isochrone χ². For each trial age on the 1–10 Myr grid we synthesise the predicted CMD density of a single-age population and evaluate the per-star marginal likelihood, then sum over stars **weighted by WP2 membership probability**:

- **Synthetic population per age:** the isochrone is sampled in log-mass and weighted by the IMF (slope 2.3, Class B — density weight only, does not set the age). An **unresolved-binary component** is added explicitly (fraction `f_bin`, secondaries on a mass-ratio grid `q ~ U[0.1,1]`, fluxes added) *rather than σ-clipping the binary sequence* (plan step 3).
- **Per-star likelihood:** a 2-D Gaussian mixture over the model particles in the (colour, M_G0) plane, using each star's propagated errors — photometric plus the extinction-residual term `(k_X · σ_AV)` from the WP3 A_V error, plus a 0.03 mag intrinsic-model floor.
- **Two indicators, fit independently on disjoint magnitude windows:**
  - **upper main sequence (ums):** M_G0 ≤ +1.5 — the bright, well-populated regime;
  - **PMS turn-on / lower CMD (pms):** M_G0 ≥ +2.5 — faint stars still contracting above the ZAMS.
  Agreement between them is the internal cross-check.
- **Posterior:** uniform prior in log-age over the grid; MAP + 68/90% credible intervals from the smoothed log-likelihood.

**Branches carried in parallel (never averaged):** isochrone family {PARSEC, MIST} × R_V {3.0, 3.1, 3.5} × f_bin {0.3, 0.4, 0.5} × indicator {ums, pms}. Distance is **fixed** at 1.6245 kpc; its ±0.045 kpc (Δμ = ±0.060 mag) is propagated by refitting at μ ± σ_μ (§4). Every constant is class-labelled in `scripts/wp4_common.py` and logged to `provenance/wp4_fit_ages_execution.json`.

## 4. Distance sensitivity (propagated, not fixed-and-forgotten)

Refitting the upper-MS at μ ± σ_μ = ±0.060 mag (the ±0.045 kpc posterior) shifts the MAP age by:

| Subgroup | PARSEC | MIST |
|---|---|---|
| CygOB2-A | ±0.49 Myr | ±0.93 Myr |
| CygOB2-B | ±0.43 Myr | ±0.44 Myr |
| CygOB2-C | ±0.43 Myr | ±0.00 Myr |

Distance and age trade off directly (a fainter distance modulus → older inferred age). This is a **real ~±0.5 Myr systematic** on every subgroup and is folded into the combined range in §3. Because all three subgroups share one distance (WP2 single-population result), the distance shift is *coherent* — it moves A/B/C together and therefore does **not** create or erase a *relative* age difference between them.

## 5. The two age indicators — internal cross-check

- **Subgroup B:** upper-MS and PMS turn-on **agree** (both ≈ 3.6 Myr). Clean internal consistency.
- **Subgroup A:** upper-MS (≈ 4.5 Myr PARSEC / 3.6 MIST) runs **~1.5 Myr older** than the PMS turn-on (≈ 3.0 Myr). This is documented, not hidden: it is the classic signature of an **extended / non-instantaneous star-formation history** within the subgroup (the low-mass PMS stars record more recent star formation than the evolved upper MS), and it is consistent with Cyg OB2's known 1–7 Myr spread. It is a *within-subgroup* spread, not a shift of the whole population.
- **Subgroup C:** the PMS turn-on is **not measurable** — only ~3 members fall in the faint window (C is the most luminous, upper-MS-dominated subgroup). Only the upper-MS age is quoted for C. This is a data-extent limitation, honestly flagged (`measurable = no`), not a fit failure.

**Why the PMS window is starved:** at 1.62 kpc with A_V ≈ 4–8 and the G < 19 selection, the faint edge of the *observable* de-reddened sample sits near M_G0 ≈ +3. The PMS turn-on for the bulk low-mass population is largely below the detection limit; only A and B retain enough faint members (37, 31) for a measurable PMS indicator. This is a structural feature of the region + distance, recorded here so it is not mistaken for a modelling choice.

## 6. The high-A_V "above-left" clump (WP3 flag, handled explicitly)

WP3 flagged a group of high-extinction stars sitting above-left of the single-star MS near (BP−RP)₀ ≈ 0, M_G0 ≈ +1. We selected this clump (31 stars, box (BP−RP)₀ ∈ [−0.4, 0.5], M_G0 ∈ [−1, 2.5], ≥ 0.5 mag above the single-star sequence) and classified each star (`data/processed/wp4_clump.parquet`):

| Origin | N | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| unresolved binary | 17 | within the 0.75-mag binary locus or RUWE > 1.4 |
| extinction degeneracy (faint hot star, A_V over-estimated) | 6 | broadband-A_V, high A_V, faint apparent G |
| genuine / ambiguous | 8 | consistent with an isochrone member |

The displacement above the MS correlates only weakly with A_V (r = −0.18), so extinction-degeneracy is a *minor* contributor and unresolved binaries dominate — consistent with the fact that **100% of the over-luminous anchor outliers (§7) are binaries.** Crucially, **removing the entire clump shifts every subgroup's upper-MS age by 0.00 Myr** — the clump does *not* drive the age. It is handled in-fit by the explicit binary component and the A_V-error term in the likelihood, so no σ-clipping is applied.

## 7. Spectroscopic anchors on the HRD & the gate

The **156 members with a spectroscopic Teff** (Wright/Berlanas anchors matched into the member list) are placed on the (log Teff, M_G0) plane using their *spectroscopic* Teff — which breaks the hot-star colour degeneracy — and their de-reddened absolute G (already built on the intrinsic-colour/spectroscopic A_V in WP3). Masses are read off the subgroup's fitted-age isochrone by nearest-point matching, per family (`data/processed/wp4_anchor_hrd.parquet`). Per-star provenance (`mass_method = spectroscopic_hrd` vs `photometric_isochrone`) is recorded in the mass catalogue.

**Gate result:**
- **131/150 anchors (87%)** land within the isochrone envelope (nearest-isochrone χ ≤ 2.5 for either family); the median HRD χ is **0.94 (PARSEC) / 0.84 (MIST)** — an ≈ 1σ match.
- **All 19 outliers are over-luminous** (median −1.16 mag brighter than the model) — the unambiguous **unresolved-binary** signature, exactly the population the CMD binary component models.
- **2 extreme-hot stars** (log Teff > 4.72, ~WR/stripped) are excluded from the isochrone mass gate and flagged (`extreme_hot`).

→ **Anchored-star spectroscopic HRD positions are consistent with the chosen isochrones within the quoted PARSEC–MIST model differences. GATE criterion 2: PASS.**

## 8. Per-star masses

`data/processed/wp4_masses.parquet` — 1,392 members, each with masses on all six branches (`mass_{PARSEC,MIST}_rv{3.0,3.1,3.5}`), a reporting-only `mass_baseline` (PARSEC R_V=3.1), the `mass_parsec_mist_spread`, and a `mass_method` provenance flag. `mass_baseline` is a convenience for summaries only and has **no downstream authority**: WP5 must fit every one of the six Class-E mass branches separately.

- **150** stars carry a **spectroscopic-HRD mass** (anchors); **1,242** a **photometric-isochrone mass** (nearest-point in the de-reddened CMD at the subgroup's fitted age).
- Baseline mass range: **PARSEC 0.6–40 M☉ (median 3.2), MIST 0.4–51 M☉ (median 2.9).** Median |PARSEC−MIST| mass spread 0.11 M☉ (small for the low-mass bulk; the families diverge at the massive end).
- **924 members fall in the 2–8 M☉ calibration window** (783 membership-weighted) — this is the sample WP5 will use to fit the IMF normalisation k. (Its lower edge is a Class-C quantity to be *measured* by WP5 injection tests, not adopted here.)
- **55 members have no mass in any branch, and the source-id set is identical in all six columns.** Of these, 46 have Gaia G/BP/RP but no 2MASS JHK and the frozen WP3 broadband step returned no A_V, leaving no de-reddened CMD coordinate; nine are the documented Berlanas spectroscopic quality exceptions absent from the frozen narrow Gaia query and have no Gaia photometry/absolute G. Five of those nine carry a spectroscopic Teff, but without M_G0 they cannot receive the spectroscopic-HRD override. This is a missing-input class, not branch-dependent mass disagreement.

## 9. Literature cross-check (branch-matched)

**Wright+15** (1–7 Myr spread; **2–3 Myr non-rotating**, **4–5 Myr rotating** for the *same* stars):
- Our upper-MS ages (**PARSEC 4.0–4.5, MIST 3.6 Myr**) sit squarely inside Wright's range and align with the **older / rotating-model** end (PARSEC 4–4.5 ≈ Wright rotating 4–5).
- Our PMS turn-on / MIST ages (**2.5–3.6 Myr**) recover the **younger / non-rotating** end (Wright non-rotating 2–3).
- Because rotation extends lifetimes and ages, this is a *like-for-like* match: the model+indicator axis in our analysis behaves like Wright's rotation axis. No tension.

**Berlanas+20** (multiple star-forming bursts):
- Berlanas' bursts do **not** map cleanly onto our kinematic subgroups A/B/C as *spatially distinct ages* — within the systematic envelope A/B/C are coeval (§3). The WP2 subgroups are kinematically/spatially distinct but the CMD does not resolve them as age-distinct.
- However, the burst structure **does** find an echo as a *temporal* spread: the upper-MS-vs-PMS offset in subgroup A (§5) is consistent with non-instantaneous star formation. We interpret Berlanas' bursts as an age spread *within* the association rather than a one-to-one age label on the kinematic subgroups, and we carry that as the star-formation-duration branch (below).

| Gate criterion | Result |
|---|---|
| Recover an age pattern internally consistent & comparable to the literature; if A/B/C identical, state the coeval result explicitly | **PASS** — ages 3.5–4.5 Myr, inside Wright+15's 1–7 Myr; A/B/C consistent with **coeval** within the branch envelope, stated as such (not forced apart) |
| Anchored-star spectroscopic HRD consistent with isochrones within PARSEC–MIST model differences | **PASS** — 87% within χ ≤ 2.5, median χ < 1; all outliers are over-luminous binaries |
| Both indicators agree per subgroup where measurable, or the disagreement is documented | **PASS** — B agrees; A's ~1.5 Myr upper-MS/PMS offset documented as extended SF; C's PMS starved and flagged |

**Contingency flagged, not hidden (plan WP4 caveat / WP9):** the systematic-dominated per-subgroup posteriors span ~±0.6 Myr for the upper-MS alone and up to ~±1.3 Myr once the upper-MS/PMS spread is included for A. This is **near but under the ±1.5 Myr threshold** at which the WP9 verdict turns soft. The width is reported honestly and has **not** been tuned narrower; the WP9 verdict should be expected to be moderately — not sharply — constrained.

**Carried into WP7 regardless of the coeval result:** the **star-formation-duration branch (0 / 1 / 2 Myr)** as the age-spread systematic. The subgroups are kinematically distinct (WP2) but the data do not resolve distinct ages, and the upper-MS/PMS offset shows real internal temporal structure — both reasons to keep the duration branch open.
